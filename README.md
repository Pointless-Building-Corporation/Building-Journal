<h1 style="text-align:center"> Building Journal </h1>

This is Building Journal, a mod that introduces a useful system for creating and trying out building quests, with verification and reward systems to encourage players to build, and build creatively within limits.

### For Players:
The Building Journal gives you commissions; a challenge that gives you a list of conditions for your own build to follow, and introduces the Builder's Compass and Drafting Table, allowing you to mark down your builds no matter how crazy or cool, and validate them with your commissions.
- While you play through Minecraft, the built-in commissions provide a nice guideline to some fun builds as you progress.
- Every 2 days, you will receive a daily commission. By completing them you can keep your completion streak alive for bragging rights.


### For Advanced Users:
The Building Journal is designed to allow for custom commissions. Anyone can create their own commissions using the [handy README](Commission_Schema_Documentation.md) and add them to their own singleplayer worlds or servers.
